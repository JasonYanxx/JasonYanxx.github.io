The presentation extended jackknife fault detection toward integrity monitoring, with emphasis on simultaneous faults, computational efficiency, and tighter protection levels under realistic non-Gaussian nominal errors. The related journal work was later published as *Jackknife ARAIM: Efficient GNSS Integrity Monitoring for Simultaneous Faults under Non-Gaussian Errors*.
